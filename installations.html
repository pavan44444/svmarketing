    Installation, done properly from start to finish

    Our team fits and commissions every system on-site, shows you how to run it, and stays in touch for service afterwards. Here is what that looks like, step by step.

    Book free water test See recent installations

    How an installation runs

    Six steps, the same every time — nothing skipped, nothing guessed.

    1. Site check

      We look at the inlet line, drain point, power point and floor space before anything is quoted.

    2. Sizing

      The system is sized from your test readings, daily consumption and number of users.

    3. Delivery

      Equipment arrives at the agreed slot and is checked against your order before it goes in.

    4. Fitting

      Plumbing, valves and connections are fitted with as few changes to your existing lines as we can manage.

    5. Commissioning

      We start up the system, check flow and settings, and test the treated water on-site.

    6. Handover & service

      We walk you through running the system and coordinate service visits after that.

    What we need from your site

    Requirements vary by system, and the site check confirms them. As a rule of thumb, this is what we look for.

    • ✓ Floor space near the inlet line for a softener or plant
    • ✓ A power point close to the system
    • ✓ A nearby drain point for softener regeneration and RO reject water
    • ✓ Access to the main inlet line for whole-house systems
    • ✓ Building or society permission, for apartment communities where it is required

    Not sure your space will work? Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll tell you.

    What changes from one property to the next

    The steps stay the same. The details do not.

    • Apartments

      Limited utility space and shared plumbing make the site check matter most. We plan around access, drainage and any building approvals.

    • Independent houses

      Whole-house softening is fitted on the main inlet line so every outlet is covered. A kitchen purifier is added separately if needed.

    • Offices & institutions

      Plant-room layouts sized to daily consumption, with drainage and service access planned before equipment arrives.

    After the installation

    A system only helps if it keeps running properly. This is what happens once we've left.

    • Handover walkthrough

      We show you how the system works, what to check and who to call if something looks off.

    • Service coordination

      We coordinate service visits so the system keeps performing. Ask us what applies to your model.

    • Consumables & spares

      Filters, membranes, UV lamps and salt needs depend on the model and your water. We tell you what yours will need.
