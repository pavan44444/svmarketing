Our Process

We start with your water.

Instead of selecting a system first, we look at the water source, application and requirement before recommending the appropriate solution.

STEP 01

Understand

We understand your property, water source and the problem you are experiencing.

STEP 02

Test

Water quality parameters such as hardness and TDS help determine the appropriate treatment category.

STEP 03

Recommend

We identify the ZeroB solution that matches your water condition and expected usage.

STEP 04

Install & Support

We coordinate installation and help you with the next steps for operating the selected system.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us your water source, property type and the problem you're facing. We'll help you identify whether you need a softener, RO purifier or UV purification solution.
