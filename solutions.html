    The technologies behind ZeroB systems

    Different problems in your water call for different technology. Here's what each one does.

    RO (Reverse Osmosis)

    Pushes water through a fine membrane to reduce dissolved solids — the standard answer for high-TDS drinking water.

    UV (Ultraviolet)

    Uses ultraviolet light to inactivate bacteria and viruses in water that's already low in dissolved solids.

    Ion-exchange softening

    Resin exchanges calcium and magnesium ions for sodium, removing the minerals that cause hardness and scale.

    Iron removal

    Targets dissolved iron in borewell water that causes staining, odour and a metallic taste before it reaches your taps.
